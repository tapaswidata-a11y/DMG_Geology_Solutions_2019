Question 79: Bieniawski's Rock Mass Rating (RMR) Parameters

Official Examination Guide • Geomechanics Classification System vs. Intact Rock Weatherability Indices
Official Examination Question
Which one of the following is NOT considered for the calculation of rock mass rating?
(a) Uniaxial compressive strength
(b) Condition of discontinuities
(c) Rock quality designation
(d) Slake durability index CORRECT ANSWER (NOT INCLUDED)
1. Executive Summary & Core Principle

The Rock Mass Rating (RMR) system, also universally known as the Geomechanics Classification, was developed by Z.T. Bieniawski (1973, 1976, 1989). It is one of the most widely utilized empirical design tools in civil and mining engineering for assessing rock mass quality, designing tunnel support systems, and evaluating rock slope and foundation stability.

The calculation of the basic and adjusted RMR value relies strictly upon six specific geotechnical parameters:

  1. Uniaxial Compressive Strength (UCS) of intact rock material (or Point Load Index $I_{s(50)}$) — Rating: 0 to 15.
  2. Rock Quality Designation (RQD) (Deere 1964) — Rating: 3 to 20.
  3. Spacing of Discontinuities — Rating: 5 to 20.
  4. Condition of Discontinuities (roughness, separation/aperture, persistence, infilling/gouge, and weathering) — Rating: 0 to 30.
  5. Groundwater Conditions (inflow rate per 10 m tunnel length, joint water pressure ratio, or moisture state) — Rating: 0 to 15.
  6. Adjustment for Discontinuity Orientation (favorable, fair, unfavorable, or very unfavorable relative to the tunnel axis, dam foundation, or slope strike) — Adjustment: 0 to -12 (tunnels), up to -25 (foundations), up to -60 (slopes).

In contrast, the Slake Durability Index ($I_{d2}$) (Franklin & Chandra 1972, ISRM 1979) measures the percentage dry weight retained by a sample of rock after two standard cycles of wetting, oven-drying, and mechanical tumbling in a slake drum. It is exclusively an index of weathering susceptibility and slaking degradation for weak, argillaceous rocks (shales, mudstones, marls). It is NOT an input parameter in the RMR formula.

Therefore, the parameter that is NOT considered in the calculation of Rock Mass Rating is (d) Slake durability index.

2. Mathematical Formulation and Parameter Weights (Bieniawski 1989)

The total adjusted RMR score is computed as:

RMR = R₁ (UCS) + R₂ (RQD) + R₃ (Spacing) + R₄ (Condition) + R₅ (Groundwater) + R₆ (Orientation Adjustment)
Parameter Measured Property Rating Range Engineering Significance
1. Strength of Intact Rock ($R_1$) Point Load Index ($>10 \text{ MPa}$) or Uniaxial Compressive Strength ($>250 \text{ MPa}$) 0 – 15 Evaluates intact rock block crushing resistance under applied overburden stresses.
2. Rock Quality Designation ($R_2$) Percentage of intact core pieces $\ge 10\text{ cm}$ in total drill core run 3 – 20 Quantifies rock mass fracturing frequency; $\text{RQD} = 90\text{--}100\%$ gives maximum rating of 20.
3. Spacing of Discontinuities ($R_3$) Perpendicular distance between joints ($>2\text{ m}$ to $<60\text{ mm}$) 5 – 20 Dictates the size of individual interlocking rock blocks within the rock mass.
4. Condition of Discontinuities ($R_4$) Persistence, aperture, roughness, infilling thickness/type, joint wall weathering 0 – 30 Highest single weighted parameter; controls shear strength along potential failure surfaces.
5. Groundwater Conditions ($R_5$) Completely dry, damp, wet, dripping, or flowing water; or pore pressure ratio 0 – 15 Quantifies effective stress reduction and lubrication along joint planes.
6. Orientation Adjustment ($R_6$) Strike and dip orientation relative to engineering excavation 0 to -12 (Tunnels)
0 to -25 (Foundations)		 Penalizes unfavorable daylighting of discontinuities dipping into excavations.

4. Systematic Option Evaluation
  • (a) Uniaxial compressive strength: CONSIDERED. Parameter 1 ($R_1$) directly evaluates UCS (or Point Load Index), with ratings from 0 to 15.
  • (b) Condition of discontinuities: CONSIDERED. Parameter 4 ($R_4$) evaluates discontinuity roughness, infilling, and weathering, carrying the largest weight (up to 30 points).
  • (c) Rock quality designation: CONSIDERED. Parameter 2 ($R_2$) directly uses Deere's RQD, with ratings from 3 to 20.
  • (d) Slake durability index: NOT CONSIDERED (CORRECT ANSWER). Slake durability ($I_{d2}$) is an independent index for shale and mudrock weathering in laboratory drums; it is absent from the RMR framework.
Exam Tip: RMR = UCS + RQD + Spacing + Condition + Groundwater ± Orientation. Slake durability is NOT in RMR! Hence, (d) is the correct answer.
