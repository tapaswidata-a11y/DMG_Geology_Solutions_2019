QUESTION 53
The void ratio of the solid mass is defined as:
(a) Volume of voids / Total volume
(b) Volume of voids / Volume of solids CORRECT ANSWER
(c) Volume of solids / Volume of voids
(d) Volume of solids / Total volume

1. Executive Summary & Answer Verification

The correct option is (b) Volume of voids / Volume of solids.

In engineering geology, soil mechanics, and geotechnical engineering (codified by Karl Terzaghi):

  • Void Ratio ($e$): Defined as the ratio of the Volume of voids ($V_v$) to the Volume of solid mineral grains ($V_s$):
    e = \frac{V_v}{V_s}
  • Porosity ($n$): In contrast, porosity is defined as the ratio of the Volume of voids ($V_v$) to the Total volume of the soil/rock mass ($V$):
    n = \frac{V_v}{V} = \frac{V_v}{V_s + V_v}

Option (a) describes porosity ($n$), whereas option (b) provides the exact definition of void ratio ($e$).

2. Detailed Geotechnical & Mathematical Derivations

2.1 The Three-Phase Soil System

A natural particulate soil or porous rock mass consists of three distinct phases:

  1. Solid Phase ($V_s$, $M_s$): Mineral grains (quartz, feldspar, clay minerals).
  2. Liquid Phase ($V_w$, $M_w$): Pore water filling interstitial spaces.
  3. Gas Phase ($V_a$, $M_a \approx 0$): Air filling unsaturated pores.

The total void volume ($V_v$) is the sum of water and air volumes:

V_v = V_w + V_a  \text{and}  V = V_s + V_v = V_s + V_w + V_a

2.2 Mathematical Interrelationship Between $e$ and $n$

To derive porosity in terms of void ratio:

n = \frac{V_v}{V} = \frac{V_v}{V_s + V_v}

Dividing numerator and denominator by $V_s$:

n = \frac{\frac{V_v}{V_s}}{1 + \frac{V_v}{V_s}} = \frac{e}{1 + e}

Conversely, to express void ratio in terms of porosity:

e = \frac{n}{1 - n}

2.3 Why Civil & Mining Engineers Prefer Void Ratio ($e$) Over Porosity ($n$)

In consolidation, settlement, and compaction analysis:

  • When a soil layer compresses under structural or overburden load, water and air are squeezed out, causing the total volume ($V$) to shrink.
  • However, the mineral grains themselves are practically incompressible—meaning $V_s$ remains constant throughout deformation.
  • Because the denominator $V_s$ in $e = V_v / V_s$ is constant, any change in void ratio ($\Delta e$) is directly proportional to the change in specimen volume ($\Delta V$):
    \frac{\Delta V}{V_0} = \frac{\Delta e}{1 + e_0} = \frac{\Delta H}{H_0}
  • Porosity ($n = V_v / V$) has a variable denominator, making settlement mathematics non-linear and cumbersome.

4. Evaluation of All Multiple Choice Options

  • (a) Volume of voids / Total volume — INCORRECT: This is the definition of Porosity ($n$), NOT void ratio.
  • (b) Volume of voids / Volume of solids — CORRECT: This is the universal, standardized definition of Void Ratio ($e = V_v / V_s$).
  • (c) Volume of solids / Volume of voids — INCORRECT: This is the inverse of void ratio ($1/e$).
  • (d) Volume of solids / Total volume — INCORRECT: Equal to $1 - n$, representing the solid volume fraction.
