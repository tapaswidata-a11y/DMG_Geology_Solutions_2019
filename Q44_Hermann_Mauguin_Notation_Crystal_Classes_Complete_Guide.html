📝 Question Statement

(b) A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3

b A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3
c A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1

✓ Correct Answer: (a) A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4 [3A₄, 4A₃, 6A₂ = 4 3 2 (Cubic); 1A₄, 4A₂ = 4 2 2 (Tetragonal); 1A₂, 2m = m m 2 (Orthorhombic); 1A₆, 6A₂ = 6 2 2 (Hexagonal)].

🖼️ Textbook Evidence: Dexter Perkins (Chapter 9 & 10)

Box 9.7: International Hermann-Mauguin Crystallographic Symbols (Dexter Perkins, Mineralogy, 3rd ed., p. 276). Rules governing point group symbols: Numbers ($2, 3, 4, 6$) denote proper rotation axes; $m$ denotes mirror planes. For cubic crystals, the second symbol represents the four 3-fold axes along the body diagonals (e.g., $432$). For tetragonal, the first position is the principal 4-fold axis along $c$ ($422$). For hexagonal, the first position is the principal 6-fold axis along $c$ ($622$). For orthorhombic, the three positions correspond to axes along $a, b, c$ ($mm2$).
"In the Hermann-Mauguin system:
• Cubic (4 3 2): Three 4-fold axes parallel to $a, b, c$, four 3-fold axes along cube body diagonals, and six 2-fold axes along edge diagonals.
• Tetragonal (4 2 2): One 4-fold principal axis along $c$, two 2-fold axes along $a_1, a_2$, and two 2-fold diagonal axes.
• Orthorhombic (m m 2): Two mutually perpendicular vertical mirror planes intersecting along a single 2-fold rotation axis.
• Hexagonal (6 2 2): One 6-fold principal axis along $c$, three 2-fold axes along $a_1, a_2, a_3$, and three intermediate 2-fold axes."
— Dexter Perkins, Mineralogy, Chapter 9 & 10, pp. 270–276

🔍 Detailed Step-by-Step Point Group Matching

A. 3A₄, 4A₃, 6A₂ ➔ 2. 4 3 2 (Isometric / Cubic Gyroidal Class) [MATCH 1]

Symmetry Elements: Three 4-fold axes of rotation ($3A_4$), four 3-fold axes of rotation ($4A_3$), six 2-fold axes of rotation ($6A_2$). Center of symmetry and mirror planes are absent ($C$ absent, $P$ absent). In the Hermann-Mauguin system, the 4-fold axes along $a, b, c$ form the 1st position ($4$), the 3-fold body diagonal axes form the 2nd position ($3$), and the 2-fold edge diagonal axes form the 3rd position ($2$), giving 4 3 2.

B. 1A₄, 4A₂ ➔ 1. 4 2 2 (Tetragonal Trapezohedral Class) [MATCH 2]

Symmetry Elements: One 4-fold principal axis along the $c$-crystallographic axis ($1A_4$), four 2-fold axes perpendicular to $c$ ($4A_2$: two along $a_1, a_2$ and two along the bisecting diagonal directions). No mirror planes ($m$) and no center of symmetry. The Hermann-Mauguin notation is 4 2 2.

C. 1A₂, 2m ➔ 3. m m 2 (Orthorhombic Pyramidal Class) [MATCH 3]

Symmetry Elements: One 2-fold rotation axis ($1A_2$, conventionally taken along $c$) and two vertical mirror planes ($2m$) intersecting along the 2-fold axis ($m \perp a$ and $m \perp b$). No horizontal mirror plane and no center of symmetry. Written as m m 2 (or $mm2$).

D. 1A₆, 6A₂ ➔ 4. 6 2 2 (Hexagonal Trapezohedral Class) [MATCH 4]

Symmetry Elements: One 6-fold principal rotation axis along $c$ ($1A_6$), six 2-fold axes perpendicular to $c$ ($6A_2$: three along $a_1, a_2, a_3$ and three intermediate diagonal axes). No mirror planes and no inversion center. Represented in Hermann-Mauguin notation as 6 2 2.

💡 Key Takeaway & Exam Strategy

Universal Hermann-Mauguin Recognition Shortcuts:
  • Presence of a 3 in the second position (e.g., $432, m\bar{3}m, 23$) $\rightarrow$ Always Cubic (Isometric) system!
  • Starting with a 4 (e.g., $422, 4/m, 4mm$) $\rightarrow$ Always Tetragonal system!
  • Starting with a 6 (e.g., $622, 6/m, 6mm$) $\rightarrow$ Always Hexagonal system!
  • Symbols composed exclusively of $2$s and $m$s (e.g., $222, mm2, 2/m 2/m 2/m$) $\rightarrow$ Always Orthorhombic system!
  • Matching: A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4 (Option a).
