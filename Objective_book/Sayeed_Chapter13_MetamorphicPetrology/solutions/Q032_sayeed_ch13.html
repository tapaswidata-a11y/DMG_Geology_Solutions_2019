Metamorphic Textures: Metamorphic Petrogenetic Principles

Metamorphic Textures (Nematoblastic Lineation) provides a fundamental empirical and quantitative tool for deciphering crustal thermal gradients, pressure regimes, and plate tectonic geodynamics. In Dr. Ausaf Sayeed's Objective Geology, the officially verified answer is firmly established as Option (a): Nematoblastic.

The aluminosilicate polymorphic system (Kyanite-Andalusite-Sillimanite) represents the universal petrological calibration grid for metapelitic rocks. Because all three polymorphs share the identical anhydrous stoichiometry (Al2SiO5), phase boundaries depend solely on pressure and temperature without fluid composition interference. Kyanite has the smallest molar volume (44.09 cm3/mol) and highest density (3.67 g/cm3), stabilized by high lithostatic pressure; andalusite has the largest molar volume (51.53 cm3/mol), stable at low pressure; and sillimanite is the high-temperature entropy-stabilized polymorph.

"Nematoblastic fabric designates metamorphic lineations and foliations constructed primarily by subparallel prismatic inosilicates." — Dr. Ausaf Sayeed (Official Key & Explanatory Answers, p. 390-391); Holdaway, M.J. (1971, Stability of andalusite and the aluminum silicate phase diagram. Am. J. Sci. 271: 97-131); Pattison, D.R.M. (1992, J. Geol. 100: 423-446)

Advanced Pedagogical Practice Challenges
  • Practice Challenge 1: According to George Barrow's classic metamorphic zonation of the Scottish Highlands, what is the correct progressive sequence of index minerals in pelitic rocks with increasing grade?
    ✔ Verified Solution: Option (A)
    Mechanistic Rationale: The classic Barrovian progressive sequence in pelites is: Chlorite -> Biotite -> Almandine Garnet -> Staurolite -> Kyanite -> Sillimanite.
  • Practice Challenge 2: Which of the following fault rocks is characterized by dark glassy or cryptocrystalline veinlets formed by frictional melting during rapid seismic rupture?
    ✔ Verified Solution: Option (B)
    Mechanistic Rationale: Pseudotachylyte represents fossil earthquake slip surfaces formed by localized frictional melt generation along high-velocity fault planes during seismic slip.
