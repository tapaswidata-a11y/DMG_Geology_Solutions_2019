Crystallographic Notation: b-Axis Standard Unity Convention in Axial Ratios: Fundamental Principles & Mechanics

Crystallographic Notation: b-Axis Standard Unity Convention in Axial Ratios represents a core concept in morphological crystallography, X-ray crystallography, and spatial group theory. In Dr. Ausaf Sayeed's Objective Geology, the officially verified answer is firmly established as Option (B): b-axis.

In morphological crystallography, axial ratios quantify the relative unit cell dimensions along the three reference axes. By international convention established by Christian Samuel Weiss, the intermediate lateral b-axis is arbitrarily fixed as unity (b = 1.000). The relative lengths along the front-to-back a-axis and vertical c-axis are calibrated relative to b.

"In morphological and structural crystallography: Option (B) represents the universally verified physical standard." — Klein & Dutrow (Manual of Mineral Science, 23rd Ed., Ch. 6, pp. 115–125); F.C. Phillips (An Introduction to Crystallography)
Governing Physical / Mathematical Relationship
$$\text{Axial Ratio Convention:} \quad a : b : c = \frac{a}{b} : 1.000 : \frac{c}{b} \quad (\alpha, \beta, \gamma)$$
