Dual Symmetry Classification: Hemimorphic and Enantiomorphic Classes: Fundamental Principles & Mechanics

Dual Symmetry Classification: Hemimorphic and Enantiomorphic Classes represents a core concept in morphological crystallography, X-ray crystallography, and spatial group theory. In Dr. Ausaf Sayeed's Objective Geology, the officially verified answer is firmly established as Option (C): Sphenoidal (class 2) / Rhombic Disphenoidal.

The crystallographic restriction theorem dictates that only 1-, 2-, 3-, 4-, and 6-fold rotational symmetries are mathematically compatible with infinite translational periodicity in 3D space lattices. A 5-fold (pentagonal) or 7-fold axis cannot tile Euclidean space without leaving gaps or overlapping.
