Metamorphic Nomenclature: Core Sedimentological Principles

Metamorphic Nomenclature (Psammite vs Pelite vs Psephite) provides a fundamental empirical and quantitative tool for basin analysis and paleoenvironmental reconstruction. In Dr. Ausaf Sayeed's Objective Geology, the officially verified answer is firmly established as Option (b): Arenaceous rocks (sandstones).

Sedimentary petrology investigates the physical, chemical, and biological processes governing the genesis, transport, deposition, and lithification of sedimentary rocks. Integrating grain-size distribution, primary structures, detrital mineralogy, and diagenetic cements allows geologists to reconstruct paleoenvironments, paleocurrents, and tectonic provenance across Earth history.

"Psammitic rocks (from Greek psammos, sand) are derived from arenaceous sandstones; pelitic rocks are derived from mudrocks; psephitic rocks from conglomerates." — Dr. Ausaf Sayeed (Official Key & Explanatory Answers, p. 371-372); Boggs, S. Folk & Ward (1957) Graphic Granulometric Indices & Environmental Significance
Statistical Parameter Mathematical Formula Numerical Range & Classification Diagnostic Depositional Environment
Graphic Mean (Mz) $$\frac{\phi_{16} + \phi_{50} + \phi_{84}}{3}$$ Reflects overall average kinetic energy of transporting medium Fluvial gravels (<0 phi) to shelf muds (>8 phi)
Inclusive Graphic Sorting (sigma_I) $$\frac{\phi_{84} - \phi_{16}}{4} + \frac{\phi_{95} - \phi_5}{6.6}$$ <0.35 phi: Very well sorted; 0.5-1.0 phi: Moderately sorted; >2.0 phi: Very poorly sorted Beach sands (<0.35 phi); Glacial till (>3.0 phi)
Inclusive Graphic Skewness (SkI) $$\frac{\phi_{16} + \phi_{84} - 2\phi_{50}}{2(\phi_{84} - \phi_{16})} + \frac{\phi_5 + \phi_{95} - 2\phi_{50}}{2(\phi_{95} - \phi_5)}$$ +0.3 to -0.3: Strongly fine-skewed to strongly coarse-skewed Beach sands: Negatively skewed; Rivers: Positively skewed
Graphic Kurtosis (KG) $$\frac{\phi_{95} - \phi_5}{2.44(\phi_{75} - \phi_{25})}$$ <0.67: Platykurtic; 0.90-1.11: Mesokurtic; >1.50: Very leptokurtic Monosource well-sorted sands: Leptokurtic

Advanced Pedagogical Practice Challenges
  • Practice Challenge 1: A sediment sample cumulative curve yields phi16 = 1.0, phi50 = 2.0, and phi84 = 3.0. According to Folk and Ward (1957), what is its Graphic Mean (Mz)?
    ✔ Verified Solution: Option (B)
    Mechanistic Rationale: Mz = (phi16 + phi50 + phi84) / 3 = (1.0 + 2.0 + 3.0) / 3 = 6.0 / 3 = 2.00 phi (medium sand grade).
  • Practice Challenge 2: Persistent backwash wave action on a marine beach winnows away fine silt and clay particles, leaving an excess of coarse grains. This process characteristically imparts to the beach sand:
    ✔ Verified Solution: Option (B)
    Mechanistic Rationale: On the phi scale, coarse particles have lower/negative values. Depletion of fines creates an asymmetric tail toward coarse phi values, producing diagnostic negative skewness.
