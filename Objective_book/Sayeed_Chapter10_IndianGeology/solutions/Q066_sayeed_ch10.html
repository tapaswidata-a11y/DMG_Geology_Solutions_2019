Deccan Volcanism: Core Stratigraphic Principles

Deccan Volcanism (Eruption Duration) constitutes an essential component of Indian historical geology and mineral resource exploration. In Dr. Ausaf Sayeed's Objective Geology, the officially verified answer is firmly established as Option (d): Cretaceous-Paleogene Boundary (~66 to 65 Ma).

The Deccan Volcanic Province (DVP) represents one of the largest continental flood basalt provinces on Earth, triggered by the passage of the Indian Plate over the Réunion mantle plume at the Cretaceous-Paleogene boundary. High-precision 40Ar/39Ar and U-Pb geochronology demonstrates that >90% of the basalt stratigraphy (Kalsubai, Lonavala, and Wai subgroups) erupted within <750,000 years, driving massive SO2/CO2 outgassing contemporaneous with the end-Cretaceous mass extinction.

"High-precision Ar-Ar and U-Pb geochronology confirms that over 90% of the Deccan basalt volume (~1 million km³) erupted rapidly within ~750,000 years across the K-Pg boundary (~66.2-65.5 Ma)." — Dr. Ausaf Sayeed (Official Key & Notes, p. 325-328); Courtillot et al. (1988); Schoene et al. (2019, Science); Sprain et al. (2019, Science); Subbarao, K.V. (Deccan Flood Basalts)

Advanced Pedagogical Practice Challenges
  • Practice Challenge 1: Why is the Talchir Formation of the Gondwana Supergroup regarded as an exceptional chronostratigraphic time-marker across the Indian Peninsula?
    ✔ Verified Solution: Option (B)
    Mechanistic Rationale: The basal Talchir Boulder Bed represents a continent-wide continental tillite deposited by an extensive Permo-Carboniferous ice sheet, providing an unambiguous isochronous chronostratigraphic datum across all Indian Gondwana basins.
  • Practice Challenge 2: Which major tectonic boundary in the Himalaya separates the high-grade metamorphic Higher Himalayan Crystalline Complex (HHC) from the underlying sedimentary Lesser Himalaya?
    ✔ Verified Solution: Option (C)
    Mechanistic Rationale: The Main Central Thrust (MCT) is a north-dipping ductile shear zone along which the high-grade Proterozoic Vaikrita crystalline thrust sheet was emplaced southward over the low-grade metasedimentary rocks of the Lesser Himalaya.
