North Odisha Geology: Core Stratigraphic Principles

North Odisha Geology (Iron Ore Group) constitutes an essential component of Indian historical geology and mineral resource exploration. In Dr. Ausaf Sayeed's Objective Geology, the officially verified answer is firmly established as Option (b): Iron Ore Supergroup of the Singhbhum-North Odisha Craton.

The Indian Precambrian shield consists of five major cratonic nuclei: Dharwar, Bastar, Singhbhum, Bundelkhand, and Aravalli. The Singhbhum Craton preserves the Older Metamorphic Group (OMG, ~3.5 Ga) and vast Banded Iron Formations (BIF) of the Iron Ore Group (IOG). The Dharwar Craton preserves the transition from Mesoarchean Sargur high-grade greenstones and TTG gneisses to Neoarchean Dharwar volcanic-sedimentary synclinoria intruded by the 2.51 Ga Closepet Granite batholith.

"Gorumahisani and Badampahar in Mayurbhanj district represent the iconic historical source of iron ore for the first steel plant of India (TISCO, Jamshedpur)." — Dr. Ausaf Sayeed (Official Key & Notes, p. 325-328); Radhakrishna, B.P. & Vaidyanadhan, R. Advanced Pedagogical Practice Challenges
  • Practice Challenge 1: Why is the Talchir Formation of the Gondwana Supergroup regarded as an exceptional chronostratigraphic time-marker across the Indian Peninsula?
    ✔ Verified Solution: Option (B)
    Mechanistic Rationale: The basal Talchir Boulder Bed represents a continent-wide continental tillite deposited by an extensive Permo-Carboniferous ice sheet, providing an unambiguous isochronous chronostratigraphic datum across all Indian Gondwana basins.
  • Practice Challenge 2: Which major tectonic boundary in the Himalaya separates the high-grade metamorphic Higher Himalayan Crystalline Complex (HHC) from the underlying sedimentary Lesser Himalaya?
    ✔ Verified Solution: Option (C)
    Mechanistic Rationale: The Main Central Thrust (MCT) is a north-dipping ductile shear zone along which the high-grade Proterozoic Vaikrita crystalline thrust sheet was emplaced southward over the low-grade metasedimentary rocks of the Lesser Himalaya.
