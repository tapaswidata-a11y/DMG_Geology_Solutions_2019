Ore Genesis: Core Economic Geology Principles

Ore Genesis (Genetic Classes and Examples Matching) provides a fundamental quantitative and exploration foundation for mineral and energy resource evaluation. In Dr. Ausaf Sayeed's Objective Geology, the officially verified answer is firmly established as Option (a): A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2.

Waldemar Lindgren's genetic classification establishes a quantitative continuum of hydrothermal ore deposition governed by decreasing temperature and lithostatic/hydrostatic confining pressure away from igneous or metamorphic heat engines. Fluid inclusions and stable isotope thermometry demonstrate that as ascending metalliferous aqueous solutions depressurize and cool, phase separation (boiling), wall-rock sulfidation, and fluid mixing trigger progressive metal precipitation along predictable paragenetic vectors.

Advanced Pedagogical Practice Challenges
  • Practice Challenge 1: In the supergene weathering profile of a copper sulfide ore body, which of the following secondary sulfide minerals marks the highest grade of copper enrichment immediately beneath the water table?
    ✔ Verified Solution: Option (A)
    Mechanistic Rationale: Chalcocite contains 79.8% metallic copper and forms the primary constituent of the supergene enrichment blanket through replacement of primary chalcopyrite and pyrite under reducing conditions.
  • Practice Challenge 2: The world's largest single deposit of stratiform bedded volcanogenic barytes (BaSO4) is located in which geological basin of India?
    ✔ Verified Solution: Option (B)
    Mechanistic Rationale: Mangampeta in the Cuddapah Basin of Andhra Pradesh contains over 70 million tonnes of bedded tuffaceous barytes formed by submarine felsic exhalative volcanism during the Proterozoic.
