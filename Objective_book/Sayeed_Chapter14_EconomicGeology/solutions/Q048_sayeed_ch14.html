Metallic Ore Deposits: Core Economic Geology Principles

Metallic Ore Deposits (Bailadila Iron Ore Formations) provides a fundamental quantitative and exploration foundation for mineral and energy resource evaluation. In Dr. Ausaf Sayeed's Objective Geology, the officially verified answer is firmly established as Option (c): Banded Hematite Quartzites (BHQ).

Banded Iron Formations (BIFs) are rhythmically laminated Precambrian marine chemical sedimentary rocks containing >=15% iron and alternating chert/quartz bands. Algoma-type BIFs developed in Archean volcano-sedimentary greenstone belts via submarine hydrothermal exhalative venting. Superior-type BIFs precipitated on broad Paleoproterozoic continental passive margins between 2.6 and 1.8 Ga following the Great Oxidation Event (GOE) when free oxygen produced by cyanobacterial photosynthesis titrated vast reservoirs of anoxic dissolved oceanic ferrous iron.

"The high-grade hematite deposits (>65% Fe) of Bailadila occur as enriched supergene-hydrothermal bodies within the Paleoproterozoic Bailadila Group Banded Hematite Quartzite (BHQ) synclinal ridges." — Dr. Ausaf Sayeed (Official Key & Explanatory Answers, p. 417-418); James, H.L. (1954, Sedimentary facies of iron-formation. Econ. Geol. 49: 235-293); Gross, G.A. (1980, A classification of iron formations. Can. Mineral. 18: 215-222)

Advanced Pedagogical Practice Challenges
  • Practice Challenge 1: In the supergene weathering profile of a copper sulfide ore body, which of the following secondary sulfide minerals marks the highest grade of copper enrichment immediately beneath the water table?
    ✔ Verified Solution: Option (A)
    Mechanistic Rationale: Chalcocite contains 79.8% metallic copper and forms the primary constituent of the supergene enrichment blanket through replacement of primary chalcopyrite and pyrite under reducing conditions.
  • Practice Challenge 2: The world's largest single deposit of stratiform bedded volcanogenic barytes (BaSO4) is located in which geological basin of India?
    ✔ Verified Solution: Option (B)
    Mechanistic Rationale: Mangampeta in the Cuddapah Basin of Andhra Pradesh contains over 70 million tonnes of bedded tuffaceous barytes formed by submarine felsic exhalative volcanism during the Proterozoic.
