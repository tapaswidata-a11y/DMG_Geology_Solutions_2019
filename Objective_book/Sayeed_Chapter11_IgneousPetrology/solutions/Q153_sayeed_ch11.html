Lamprophyres: Core Petrological Mechanics

Lamprophyres (Minette) represents a pivotal concept in magma genesis, differentiation, and classification. In Dr. Ausaf Sayeed's Objective Geology, the officially verified answer is firmly established as Option (d): Minette.

Lamprophyres are melanocratic hypabyssal porphyritic rocks characterized by panidiomorphic (automorphic) textures containing phenocrysts exclusively of mafic minerals (biotite/phlogopite, amphibole, clinopyroxene) set in a fine-grained groundmass of feldspars and/or feldspathoids. Feldspars never form phenocrysts. They represent volatile-rich (H2O, CO2, F, Cl), high-Mg, high-LILE primitive melts derived from metasomatized subcontinental lithospheric mantle.

"Minette: biotite + orthoclase. Vogesite: hornblende + orthoclase. Kersantite: biotite + plagioclase. Spessartite: hornblende + plagioclase." — Dr. Ausaf Sayeed (Official Key & Notes, p. 344-347); Rock, N.M.S. (1991, Lamprophyres. Blackie & Son); Streckeisen, A. (1979, Classification and nomenclature of volcanic rocks, lamprophyres, carbonatites, and melilitic rocks. Geology 7: 331-335)
Governing Petrological / Thermodynamic Relationship
$$\text{Lamprophyre Classification: } \begin{cases} \text{Calc-Alkaline: } \text{Biotite/Phlogopite} + \text{K-spar (Minette) or Plagioclase (Kersantite)} \\ \text{Calc-Alkaline: } \text{Hornblende} + \text{K-spar (Vogesite) or Plagioclase (Spessartite)} \\ \text{Alkaline: } \text{Camptonite (Plag + Amph), Monchiquite (Foids/Glass)} \end{cases}$$

Advanced Pedagogical Practice Challenges
  • Practice Challenge 1: A porphyritic hypabyssal igneous rock displays prominent phenocrysts of phlogopite and diopside within a groundmass dominated by orthoclase, with zero feldspar phenocrysts. This rock is classified as a:
    ✔ Verified Solution: Option (C)
    Mechanistic Rationale: By definition in the IUGS classification, a calc-alkaline lamprophyre with biotite/phlogopite phenocrysts and an alkali feldspar (orthoclase) matrix is a minette.
  • Practice Challenge 2: What is the defining textural hallmark of all true lamprophyres under thin-section microscopy?
    ✔ Verified Solution: Option (B)
    Mechanistic Rationale: Lamprophyres exhibit a diagnostic panidiomorphic texture wherein euhedral phenocrysts consist exclusively of dark ferromagnesian minerals (biotite, amphibole, pyroxene), while feldspars are strictly confined to the groundmass.
