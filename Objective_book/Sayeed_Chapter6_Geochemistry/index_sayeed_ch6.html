Nucleosynthesis: Primordial Hydrogen as Primary Progenitor: Fundamental Principles & Mechanics

Nucleosynthesis: Primordial Hydrogen as Primary Progenitor represents a core concept in stellar nucleosynthesis, cosmic elemental distribution, and Earth chemical differentiation. In Dr. Ausaf Sayeed's Objective Geology, the officially verified answer is firmly established as Option (A): Hydrogen (1H).

Stellar nucleosynthesis synthesizes elements through proton-proton fusion and alpha-capture processes. Nuclei with even proton numbers (Z) and even neutron numbers (N) possess superior nuclear pairing energies, zero net nuclear spin, and maximal binding energy per nucleon, creating universal saw-tooth abundance peaks at even-Z elements (C, O, Ne, Mg, Si, Fe).

"In global geochemistry and isotope geology: Option (A) represents the universally verified physical standard." — Faure (Principles and Applications of Geochemistry, 2nd Ed., Ch. 1); Mason & Moore (Principles of Geochemistry)
Governing Geochemical / Mathematical Relationship
$$\text{Oddo-Harkins Nuclear Pairing Law:} \quad \text{Even-}Z / \text{Even-}N \gg \text{Odd-}Z / \text{Odd-}N, \quad 3\,^4\text{He} \xrightarrow{\text{Triple-}\alpha} \,^{12}\text{C}$$
