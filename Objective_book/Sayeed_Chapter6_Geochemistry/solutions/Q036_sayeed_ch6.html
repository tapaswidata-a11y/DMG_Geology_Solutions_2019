High-Pressure Silica: Stishovite Octahedral Coordination represents a core concept in stellar nucleosynthesis, cosmic elemental distribution, and Earth chemical differentiation. In Dr. Ausaf Sayeed's Objective Geology, the officially verified answer is firmly established as Option (B): Octahedral coordination (CN = 6, with Si coordinated by 6 oxygens in rutile structure).

The Pauling electronegativity difference between Silicon (chi = 1.90) and Oxygen (chi = 3.44) is Delta_chi = 1.54, yielding an Si-O bond that is approximately 50% covalent and 50% ionic.
