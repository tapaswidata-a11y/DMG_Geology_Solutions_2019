Colloidal Geochemistry: Particle Size Range: Fundamental Principles & Mechanics

Colloidal Geochemistry: Particle Size Range represents a core concept in stellar nucleosynthesis, cosmic elemental distribution, and Earth chemical differentiation. In Dr. Ausaf Sayeed's Objective Geology, the officially verified answer is firmly established as Option (B): 10^-7 to 10^-5 cm (1 nanometer to 100 nanometers = 10^-9 to 10^-7 meters).

Colloidal systems occupy the intermediate thermodynamic state between true molecular solutions and macroscopic suspensions. High specific surface area and net electrical surface charges (zeta potential) govern flocculation, ion adsorption, and transport of iron, aluminum, and clay mineral sols across river-estuary salinity gradients.
