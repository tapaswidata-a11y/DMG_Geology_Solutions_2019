Question 95: Mineral Stoichiometry & Silica Saturation

Detailed Chemical Derivation, Binary Phase Equilibria, and Mutual Incompatibility of Nepheline and Quartz

Q.95: The chemical formula of which one of the following minerals can be expressed as a combination of nepheline and quartz?

(a) Anorthoclase
(b) Analcime
(c) Antigorite
✓ (d) Albite
Correct Option: (d) Albite

1. Stoichiometric Derivation

In mineralogy and petrology, silica saturation defines whether a rock or mineral contains sufficient SiO2 to form fully saturated framework silicates (feldspars) or deficient SiO2 requiring feldspathoids:

  • Nepheline (Feldspathoid): An undersaturated sodium tectosilicate with the formula NaAlSiO4.
  • Quartz (Silica): Pure framework silica with the formula SiO2.
  • Albite (Alkali Feldspar): Saturated sodium tectosilicate with the formula NaAlSi3O8.
Stoichiometric Combination:

  NaAlSiO4 (Nepheline) + 2 SiO2 (Quartz) → NaAlSi3O8 (Albite)

• Sodium (Na): 1 = 1
• Aluminium (Al): 1 = 1
• Silicon (Si): 1 + 2 = 3
• Oxygen (O): 4 + (2 × 2) = 8

The sum of one formula unit of nepheline and two formula units of quartz yields exactly one formula unit of albite.

2. Petrological Significance: The Thermal Divide

In the binary system NaAlSiO4–SiO2 (Bowen, 1937; Schairer & Yoder, 1960), albite melts congruently at 1118°C at atmospheric pressure. Albite forms a high-temperature thermal divide (or thermal barrier):

  • Magmas with bulk composition to the right of albite (excess SiO2) fractionate towards the quartz-albite eutectic (e.g., granites, rhyolites).
  • Magmas with bulk composition to the left of albite (deficient in SiO2) fractionate towards the nepheline-albite eutectic (e.g., nepheline syenites, phonolites).
  • Because the free energy of reaction ΔG for Nepheline + 2 Quartz → Albite is strongly negative under crustal conditions, nepheline and quartz can never coexist in thermodynamic equilibrium in igneous or metamorphic rocks. Any excess silica immediately reacts with nepheline to synthesize albite.

3. Systematic Option Evaluation

Option Mineral Name Chemical Formula Evaluation & Relationship to Nepheline + Quartz
(a) Anorthoclase (Na,K)AlSi3O8 Incorrect. Contains essential potassium (solid solution between albite and sanidine, typically Or10Ab90 to Or36Ab64); cannot be formed purely from pure sodium nepheline and quartz.
(b) Analcime NaAlSi2O6·H2O Incorrect. A hydrous zeolite/feldspathoid. Stoichiometrically represents Nepheline + Quartz + H2O, but the anhydrous combination of nepheline and quartz yields albite.
(c) Antigorite Mg3Si2O5(OH)4 Incorrect. A magnesium-rich serpentine-group phyllosilicate found in hydrated ultramafic rocks; contains Mg and OH, completely devoid of Na and Al.
(d) Albite NaAlSi3O8 Correct. Pure anhydrous framework silicate formed by combining 1 mole of nepheline (NaAlSiO4) with 2 moles of quartz (SiO2).

Key Petrological Takeaway

Feldspathoids like nepheline (NaAlSiO4) and leucite (KAlSi2O6) are silica-undersaturated equivalents of the alkali feldspars albite (NaAlSi3O8) and K-feldspar (KAlSi3O8). The presence of modal quartz immediately precludes the presence of modal nepheline in any equilibrium assemblage.
