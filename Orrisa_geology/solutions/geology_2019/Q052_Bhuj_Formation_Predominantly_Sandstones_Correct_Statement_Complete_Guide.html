Sedimentary Basin Analysis: Bhuj Formation & Proterozoic Purana Basins

Forensic evaluation of sedimentary successions in competitive examinations requires precise familiarity with diagnostic lithofacies across both Phanerozoic pericratonic rift basins (such as the Kutch Basin) and Proterozoic intracratonic Purana basins (Vindhyan, Cuddapah, Kaladgi, and Bhima).

  • Forensic Assessment of Statement (A) — The Mesozoic Bhuj Formation:
    • Stratigraphic Position: The Bhuj Formation (traditionally mapped as the Upper Umia beds or Bhuj Sandstone) represents the uppermost Mesozoic lithostratigraphic unit of the Kutch Mainland, spanning the Lower Cretaceous (Tithonian-Neocomian to Aptian/Albian).
    • Lithological Character: The formation reaches over $300 - 800\text{ m}$ thickness and consists predominantly of white, pale yellow, and pink, medium- to coarse-grained quartzose sandstones. These sandstones exhibit giant cross-stratification (planar and trough cross-bedding), ripple marks, and channel scour-and-fill structures, interbedded with subordinate siltstones and plant-bearing carbonaceous shales (Ptilophyllum flora).
    • Depositional Environment: Fluvial braided river to deltaic plain conditions, reflecting the gradual withdrawal of the Jurassic Tethyan sea and progradation of large sandy delta systems over the Kutch shelf.
  • Refutation of Incorrect Distractor Statements:
    • Statement (B) — Kaladgi Basin: The Kaladgi Basin (Karnataka) is dominated by unmetamorphosed to weakly metamorphosed quartz arenites (Saundatti quartzite), conglomerates, stromatolitic limestones, and dolomites (Bagalkot and Badami Groups). It is not predominantly made of slates.
    • Statement (C) — Vindhyan Supergroup: The claim that the Vindhyan Supergroup is 'devoid of limestone' is completely absurd. The Vindhyan is India's greatest carbonate storehouse, featuring monumental limestone formations (Kajrahat, Rohtas, Lakheri, Bhander) that support the majority of India's cement manufacturing plants.
    • Statement (D) — Cuddapah Basin: The Cuddapah Basin is predominantly made of sedimentary siliciclastics and carbonates (Gulcheru quartzite, Vempalle dolomite, Pulivendla quartzite, Tadpatri shale, Bairenkonda quartzite, Cumbum shale). Metadacites are extremely minor local volcanic flows; the basin is emphatically not predominantly metadacite.
"Bhuj Formation: In Kutch, the Umia beds pass upward into a thick succession of sandstones known as the Bhuj Formation. It consists predominantly of massive, cross-bedded sandstones with thin intercalations of shales containing plant fossils. These sandstones are extensively quarried for building stones throughout Gujarat." — K.M. Bangar, Principles of Engineering Geology (Cretaceous System: Bhuj Beds, p. 297)

OPSC Assistant Geologist 2019 • Paper-II • Question 52
Which of the following statement is correct ?
(A) The Mesozoic Bhuj formation consists predominantly of sandstones
(B) Kaladgi basin is predominantly made of slates
(C) Vindhyan supergroup is devoid of limestone
(D) Cuddapah basin is predominantly made of metadacites
✔ OFFICIALLY VERIFIED ANSWER: (A) The Mesozoic Bhuj formation consists predominantly of sandstones
(A) The Mesozoic Bhuj formation consists predominantly of sandstones CORRECT VERIFIED
Forensic Analysis: The Mesozoic Bhuj Formation (Lower Cretaceous of Kutch) is a textbook fluvial-deltaic succession reaching hundreds of meters thickness, composed overwhelmingly of friable to well-cemented quartzose sandstones featuring cross-bedding and channel scour-and-fills. This statement is physically and stratigraphically accurate.
(B) Kaladgi basin is predominantly made of slates INCORRECT
Forensic Analysis: The Kaladgi Basin is a Proterozoic epicontinental basin dominated by quartzites, conglomerates, cherts, and massive stromatolitic dolomites and limestones (Bagalkot Group). Slates represent only localized, minor argillaceous facies in deformed zones; the basin is not predominantly slates.
(C) Vindhyan supergroup is devoid of limestone INCORRECT
Forensic Analysis: The Vindhyan Supergroup is world-famous for containing massive, high-grade limestone formations (Kajrahat, Rohtas, Lakheri, Bhander). It supplies the vast majority of cement manufacturing raw material in Central India, making this assertion diametrically opposite to fact.
(D) Cuddapah basin is predominantly made of metadacites INCORRECT
Forensic Analysis: The Cuddapah Basin is a crescent-shaped Purana sedimentary basin composed of quartzites, shales, and carbonate formations. Felsic to intermediate metadacite volcanic flows occur only as minor, localized volcanic intercalations (Tadpatri volcanic episode); the basin is overwhelmingly sedimentary.

8 Fully Worked Practice Problems: Indian Sedimentary Basins & Lithofacies
Problem 1: Paleocurrent Indicators in Bhuj Sandstone
Trough cross-bedding and parting lineations indicating westerly to north-westerly flow
Directional sedimentary structures in the Bhuj Formation reveal fluvial transport from the Precambrian Aravalli highlands westward into the expanding Early Cretaceous proto-oceanic gulf.
Problem 2: The Two Groups of the Kaladgi Basin
Bagalkot Group (deformed lower succession) and Badami Group (flat-lying upper succession)
The Kaladgi basin records an older, folded marine platform sequence (Bagalkot: Lokapur and Simikeri subgroups) overlain unconformably by the horizontally bedded arenaceous Badami Group.
Problem 3: Economic Limestone Units of the Vindhyan Supergroup
Rohtas Limestone (Semri Group) and Bhander / Lakheri Limestone (Bhander Group)
The Rohtas Limestone consists of fine-grained micritic limestones yielding >85% CaCO3, quarried in massive open-cast mines across Bihar, MP, and Rajasthan.
Problem 4: Chrysotile Asbestos and Barytes Host Formation in Cuddapah
Vempalle Formation (Papaghni Group)
The Vempalle dolomitic limestones, where intruded by basic dolerite sills, underwent serpentinization producing high-grade chrysotile asbestos and vein barytes (Pulivendla belt).
Problem 5: Ptilophyllum Flora in the Bhuj Formation
Identified in carbonaceous shale interbeds confirming Early Cretaceous age
The presence of cycadeoid fronds (*Ptilophyllum acutifolium*, *Otozamites*) establishes biostratigraphic equivalence between the Bhuj Formation and the Rajmahal/Jabalpur plant beds.
Problem 6: Mangampet Barytes Deposit in Cuddapah Basin
World's largest bedded volcanogenic barytes deposit hosted in the Pullampet (Cumbum) shales
The Mangampet deposit formed by submarine exhalative (sedex) volcanic venting of barium-rich hydrothermal fluids into sulfidic shale lagoons.
Problem 7: Capping Unit Resting Above the Bhuj Formation
Deccan Traps (Late Cretaceous basalt flows)
The Bhuj Formation is unconformably overlain by subaerial basaltic lava flows of the Deccan Traps, which were extruded during the passage of India over the Reunion hotspot.
Problem 8: Glauconite Sandstones in the Vindhyan Supergroup
Occurs in the Semri Group (Koldaha shale) and Kaimur Group
Glauconite pellets in Vindhyan sandstones indicate shallow, open-marine shelf deposition under reducing micro-environments during slow sedimentation intervals.
