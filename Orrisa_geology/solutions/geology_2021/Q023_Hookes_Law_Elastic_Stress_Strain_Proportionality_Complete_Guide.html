Q23: Rock Mechanics: Hooke's Law of Linear Elastic Deformation

Exam: Geology Paper-II (2021)  |  Authoritative Reference: Haakon Fossen (Structural Geology, Ch. 6, pp. 110-115); Turcotte & Schubert (Geodynamics, Ch. 3)

Part I: Theoretical Foundation & Concept Masterclass

23.1 Fundamental Rheological Models of Rock Deformation

In continuum mechanics, rock mechanics, and structural geology (Haakon Fossen, Structural Geology, Ch. 6, pp. 110–115; Turcotte & Schubert, Geodynamics, Ch. 3), the mechanical response of a rock mass under applied stress ($\sigma$) is described by constitutive rheological laws:

Constitutive Law Mathematical Formulation Physical Behavior & Rock Rheology
Hooke's Law (Elasticity) $\sigma = E \cdot \epsilon$ Linear elastic behavior: Strain is directly proportional to stress; instantaneous and 100% recoverable upon stress release.
Newton's Law (Viscosity) $\sigma = \eta \cdot \dot{\epsilon}$ Viscous fluid flow: Stress is proportional to strain rate; permanent non-recoverable deformation.
St. Venant (Plasticity) $\sigma = \sigma_y$ Plastic yield: Permanent deformation occurs only when stress exceeds a critical yield stress.

23.2 Generalized 3D Hooke's Law

In isotropic three-dimensional rock mechanics, Hooke's law relates stress and strain through Young's Modulus ($E$) and Poisson's Ratio ($\nu$):

$$\epsilon_x = \frac{1}{E} [\sigma_x - \nu(\sigma_y + \sigma_z)]$$ $$\epsilon_y = \frac{1}{E} [\sigma_y - \nu(\sigma_x + \sigma_z)]$$ $$\epsilon_z = \frac{1}{E} [\sigma_z - \nu(\sigma_x + \sigma_y)]$$

Part II: Official Question & Critical Option Dissection

Q23. In elastic deformation, when strain is proportional to stress is known as :
(A) Smith's law
(B) Poisson's ratio
(C) Hooke’s law
(D) None of these
VERIFIED ANSWER: OPTION (C)

Correct Answer: (C) Hooke’s law — In elastic deformation, the linear proportionality between stress and strain ($\sigma = E \cdot \epsilon$) is known as Hooke’s law.

Option-by-Option Critical Dissection

  • (A) Smith's law — INCORRECT: No such constitutive physical law in rock mechanics.
  • (B) Poisson's ratio — INCORRECT: Poisson's ratio is a dimensionless constant ($ u = -\epsilon_{ ext{trans}} / \epsilon_{ ext{axial}}$) measuring lateral expansion, not the proportionality law itself.
  • (C) Hooke’s law — CORRECT: Formulated by Robert Hooke in 1678 (ut tensio, sic vis), established as the foundational law of linear elasticity.
  • (D) None of these — INCORRECT.

Part III: Synthesis Reference Tables & High-Yield Practice

Rock Elastic Moduli Calculation Problem

Practice Problem 23: Young's Modulus & Elastic Shortening

A cylindrical granite core of length $L_0 = 100\text{ mm}$ with Young's Modulus $E = 50\text{ GPa}$ is subjected to a uniaxial compressive stress of $\sigma = 150\text{ MPa}$. Calculate the axial elastic strain ($\epsilon$) and change in length ($\Delta L$).

Solution:

$$\epsilon = \frac{\sigma}{E} = \frac{150 \times 10^6\text{ Pa}}{50 \times 10^9\text{ Pa}} = 0.003 = 0.3\%$$ $$\Delta L = \epsilon \cdot L_0 = 0.003 \times 100\text{ mm} = 0.3\text{ mm}$$

The granite core shortens elastically by $0.3\text{ mm}$, which will be completely recovered if the stress is released.
