  • (A) Unconfined aquifer - Semi-confined aquifer - Confined aquifer
  • (B) Semiconfined aquifer - Confined aquifer - Unconfined aquifer

2. Detailed Geological Masterclass: Storage Coefficient ($S$) across Aquifer Types

The Storage Coefficient (Storativity, $S$) is defined as the volume of water an aquifer releases from or takes into storage per unit surface area of aquifer per unit change in hydraulic head:

Aquifer Type Water Release Mechanism Typical Storage Coefficient ($S$)
1. Confined Aquifer Water is released solely via elastic compression of the aquifer mineral skeleton and elastic expansion of the water itself under decreasing fluid pressure: $$S = S_s \cdot b = \rho_w g (\alpha + n \beta) b$$ $10^{-5} - 10^{-3}$ (Extremely small, dimensionless)
2. Semi-Confined (Leaky) Aquifer Elastic release augmented by vertical leakage of water across semi-permeable confining aquitards: $10^{-3} - 10^{-1}$ (Intermediate)
3. Unconfined (Water Table) Aquifer Water is released primarily by actual physical gravity drainage of pore spaces as the water table declines; storativity equals Specific Yield ($S_y$): $$S \approx S_y$$ $0.05 - 0.30$ ($5\% - 30\%$; thousands of times larger than confined aquifers)

Arranged in order of increasing value: $$\textbf{Confined } (10^{-5}) \; < \; \textbf{Semi-confined } (10^{-2}) \; < \; \textbf{Unconfined } (0.20)$$ This precisely matches Option (C).

3. Critical Option Elimination & Exam Traps

  • Option C is correct: Confined ($10^{-5}$) < Semi-confined ($10^{-2}$) < Unconfined ($10^{-1}$).
  • Option A: Arranged in decreasing order, the opposite of the question prompt!
  • Option B & D: Confined aquifers possess the lowest storativity of all, so they must come first in an increasing series.
