Question 109: Amongst the following which is the highest grade of metamorphic rock ?

  • (A) Talc-schist
  • (B) Garnet-schist
  • (C) Mica-schist
  • (D) Chlorite-schist

Part II: Official Question & Critical Option Dissection

Q109. Amongst the following which is the highest grade of metamorphic rock ?
(A) Talc-schist
(B) Garnet-schist
(C) Mica-schist
(D) Chlorite-schist

2. Detailed Geological Masterclass: The Barrovian Zonal Sequence

George Barrow (1893, 1912) established the classic sequence of metamorphic index minerals in pelitic rocks of the Scottish Highlands under intermediate-pressure regional metamorphism:

Barrovian Zone Diagnostic Metamorphic Mineral Metamorphic Grade & Facies Approximate Temperature ($T$)
1. Chlorite Zone Chlorite + Muscovite + Albite Low Grade (Greenschist facies) $300^\circ - 350^\circ\text{C}$
2. Biotite Zone Biotite + Chlorite + Muscovite Low Grade (Greenschist facies) $350^\circ - 450^\circ\text{C}$
3. Garnet Zone Almandine Garnet + Biotite + Muscovite Medium-to-High Grade (Amphibolite facies) $450^\circ - 550^\circ\text{C}$
4. Staurolite Zone Staurolite + Garnet + Biotite Medium Grade (Amphibolite facies) $550^\circ - 600^\circ\text{C}$
5. Kyanite Zone Kyanite + Garnet + Biotite High Grade (Amphibolite facies) $600^\circ - 650^\circ\text{C}$
6. Sillimanite Zone Sillimanite + K-feldspar / Quartz High Grade (Upper Amphibolite / Granulite) $> 650^\circ\text{C}$

Talc-schist is a hydrated ultrabasic metamorphic rock of the greenschist facies ($300-400^\circ\text{C}$). Chlorite-schist marks the lowest greenschist grade ($300^\circ\text{C}$). General mica-schist spans greenschist to lower amphibolite. Therefore, Garnet-schist represents the highest metamorphic grade among the listed choices.

Part III: Synthesis Reference Tables & High-Yield Practice

3. Critical Option Elimination & Exam Traps

  • Option B (Garnet-schist) is correct: The appearance of almandine garnet marks the transition into the amphibolite facies ($T \approx 450-550^\circ\text{C}$), the highest grade in the list.
  • Option D (Chlorite-schist): Low-grade greenschist facies ($300^\circ\text{C}$).
  • Option A (Talc-schist): Low-grade greenschist alteration of ultramafic protoliths.
  • Option C (Mica-schist): Broad category; when unqualified, it precedes or encompasses the biotite zone prior to garnet entry.
