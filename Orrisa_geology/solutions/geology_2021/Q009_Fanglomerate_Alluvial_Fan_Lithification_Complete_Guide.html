Q9: Fanglomerate Lithification & Alluvial Fan Facies

Exam: Geology Paper-II (2021)  |  Authoritative Reference: Sam Boggs Jr. (Sedimentology & Stratigraphy, Ch. 5, pp. 152-156); Pettijohn (Sedimentary Rocks, 3rd Ed.)

Part I: Theoretical Foundation & Concept Masterclass

9.1 Origin and Definition of "Fanglomerate"

In sedimentary petrology (Sam Boggs Jr., Principles of Sedimentology and Stratigraphy, 5th Ed., Ch. 5; F.J. Pettijohn, Sedimentary Rocks, 3rd Ed.), the term Fanglomerate was coined by A.C. Lawson (1913) to designate the lithified, consolidated rock equivalent of alluvial fan gravels and debris flows.

Term Geological Definition & Nature
Fanglomerate Lithified alluvial fan conglomerate; characterized by extreme textural immaturity, poor sorting, polymodal grain size, angular to sub-rounded clasts, and matrix-supported or clast-supported fabrics.
Bajada A geomorphic landform (not a lithified rock) consisting of a continuous apron formed by the lateral coalescence of adjacent alluvial fans along a mountain front.
Agglomerate A pyroclastic volcanic rock composed predominantly of coarse, angular or rounded volcanic bombs ($> 64\text{ mm}$) ejected during explosive volcanism.
Orthoconglomerate A texturally mature, clast-supported conglomerate with $< 15\%$ fine matrix, typically well-rounded and deposited by turbulent fluvial channel currents.

Part II: Official Question & Critical Option Dissection

Q9. The lithified portions of alluvial fans are termed as :
(A) Bajada
(B) Agglomerate
(C) Fanglomerate
(D) Orthoconglomerate
VERIFIED ANSWER: OPTION (C)

Correct Answer: (C) Fanglomerate — The lithified portion of an alluvial fan is strictly classified in sedimentary petrology as a Fanglomerate.

Option-by-Option Critical Dissection

  • (A) Bajada — INCORRECT: Bajada is a geomorphic depositional surface (a compound alluvial fan apron), not a consolidated rock type.
  • (B) Agglomerate — INCORRECT: Agglomerate is an igneous pyroclastic rock formed of volcanic bombs, not alluvial sediment.
  • (C) Fanglomerate — CORRECT: The classical, authoritative sedimentary rock designation for consolidated alluvial fan deposits.
  • (D) Orthoconglomerate — INCORRECT: Orthoconglomerates are stream-deposited, clast-supported conglomerates with minimal matrix, not debris-flow dominated fan facies.

Sedimentology Practice Problem

Practice Problem 9: Paraconglomerate vs. Orthoconglomerate

How does a fanglomerate deposited by an alluvial fan debris flow classify under Pettijohn's matrix-clast fabric scheme?

Answer:

It classifies as a Paraconglomerate (matrix-supported conglomerate, $> 15\%$ matrix), because laminar debris flow slurries transport boulders suspended within a cohesive mud-sand-water matrix.
