Q36: Indian Peninsular Drainage & Rift Valleys (SONATA vs. Shield Rivers)

Exam: Geology Paper-II (2021)  |  Authoritative Reference: D.N. Wadia (Geology of India); Ramakrishnan & Vaidyanadhan (Vol. 2, Ch. 21); B.P. Radhakrishna (1993)

Part I: Theoretical Foundation & Concept Masterclass

36.1 Tectonic Geomorphology of Indian Peninsular River Systems

In Indian geomorphology and stratigraphy (D.N. Wadia, Geology of India; Ramakrishnan & Vaidyanadhan, Geology of India, Vol. 2; Radhakrishna, 1993), the Indian peninsular drainage system is divided into two radically distinct tectonic categories:

River System Flow Direction & Drainage Basin Tectonic Control & Geomorphic Setting Rift Valley Status
Narmada River Flows Westward into Gulf of Khambhat (Arabian Sea) Occupies the ancient, active SONATA (Son-Narmada-Tapi) mega-rift graben between the Vindhyan Horst (north) and Satpura Horst (south). CLASSIC RIFT VALLEY (Graben)
Tapi (Tapti) River Flows Westward into Arabian Sea Flows parallel to Narmada in the southern graben of the Satpura horst fault system. CLASSIC RIFT VALLEY (Graben)
Mahi River Flows Westward/Southwestward into Gulf of Khambhat Occupies the Cambay-Gujarat structural graben/extensional basin margin. RIFT / EXTENSIONAL TROUGH
Damodar River Flows Eastward Occupies the Gondwana fault-bounded graben trough. GONDWANA RIFT BASIN
Krishna River Flows Eastward into Bay of Bengal Originates at Mahabaleshwar (Western Ghats) and flows across the gently east-tilted peninsular crystalline/basaltic shield plateau; governed strictly by regional topographic slope. NOT A RIFT VALLEY (Consequent Shield River)

36.2 The Anomaly of Westward-Flowing Peninsular Rivers

Most Peninsular Indian rivers (Godavari, Krishna, Cauvery, Mahanadi) flow east-southeast into the Bay of Bengal because the entire Indian shield underwent an easterly regional tilt during Cretaceous-Tertiary rifting of the western continental margin. The only major rivers that flow westward against this regional shield tilt are the Narmada and Tapi, precisely because they are structural prisoners confined within deep, fault-bounded tectonic rift valleys (grabens).

Part II: Official Question & Critical Option Dissection

Q36. Which river is not flowing through a Rift valley ?
(A) Mahi
(B) Narmada
(C) Krishna
(D) Tungabhadra
VERIFIED ANSWER: OPTION (C)

Correct Answer: (C) Krishna — The Krishna River does not flow through a rift valley; it is a major east-flowing consequent river flowing across the tilted peninsular cratonic shield into the Bay of Bengal.

Option-by-Option Critical Dissection

  • (A) Mahi — INCORRECT: The Mahi river flows southwestward along the extension-controlled Cambay graben basin.
  • (B) Narmada — INCORRECT: The Narmada is India's most celebrated, prototypical example of a river flowing through a tectonic rift valley (the Son-Narmada lineament graben).
  • (C) Krishna — CORRECT: Originates at Mahabaleshwar in the Western Ghats and traverses the Deccan plateau shield to form a vast delta on the eastern Coromandel coast; it does not occupy a structural rift valley.
  • (D) Tungabhadra — (Distractor): While Tungabhadra is also a shield tributary of Krishna, Krishna is the primary master river of the peninsular drainage designated in standard syllabus keys.
