2. Detailed Geological Masterclass: Aquifer Textural Parameters and Well Yield

The hydraulic conductivity ($K$), specific yield, and well screen design of an unconsolidated sand aquifer are strictly dictated by two granulometric parameters derived from sieve analysis:

  1. Effective Grain Size ($D_{10}$):
    • The sieve diameter through which $10\%$ of the sediment mass passes.
    • According to Hazen's formula ($K = C \cdot D_{10}^2$), hydraulic conductivity is directly proportional to the square of effective size. A larger $D_{10}$ signifies substantially larger pore throat diameters, drastically reducing frictional resistance to fluid flow.
    • Comparing the candidate zones, $D_{10} = 0.75\text{ mm}$ (Zones II and IV) is far superior to $0.55\text{ mm}$ and $0.65\text{ mm}$.
  2. Uniformity Coefficient ($C_u$):
    • Defined as $C_u = \frac{D_{60}}{D_{10}}$.
    • $C_u$ measures the sorting and gradation of the sand. A lower $C_u$ ($< 2.5$) indicates a highly uniform, well-sorted sand where grain sizes are nearly identical.
    • A higher $C_u$ ($> 3.5$) indicates poorly sorted, well-graded sand where smaller particles fill the voids between larger grains, dramatically choking porosity and choking hydraulic permeability!

Synthesis for Zone Selection:
Both Zone II and Zone IV have the highest effective size ($D_{10} = 0.75\text{ mm}$). However, Zone II has $C_u = 2.10$ (well-sorted, open packing, high permeability), whereas Zone IV has $C_u = 3.70$ (poorly sorted, interstitial clogging by finer grains). Therefore, Zone II is unequivocally the most promising aquifer zone for groundwater withdrawal.

3. Critical Option Elimination & Exam Traps

  • Option B (Zone II) is correct: Combines maximum coarse effective size ($D_{10} = 0.75\text{ mm}$) with superior sorting ($C_u = 2.10$).
  • Option D (Zone IV): While $D_{10}$ is $0.75\text{ mm}$, high $C_u = 3.70$ indicates poorly sorted grains with fine matrix clogging pore channels.
  • Option A & C: Substantially smaller effective grain sizes ($0.55\text{ mm}$ and $0.65\text{ mm}$).
