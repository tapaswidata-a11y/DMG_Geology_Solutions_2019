Q58: Precambrian Stratigraphy of Rajasthan - Aravalli Mountain Belt Succession

Exam: Geology Paper-II (2021)  |  Authoritative Reference: G.B. Mahapatra (A Text-Book of Geology, p. 312); P.K. Mukherjee (A Text Book of Geology, p. 200)

Part I: Theoretical Foundation & Concept Masterclass

1. Regional Architecture of the Northwestern Indian Craton

The Aravalli Mountain Belt in Rajasthan and northern Gujarat represents one of the oldest and most spectacular Proterozoic orogenic belts on Earth, extending over 700 km from Delhi south-southwest to Champaner. Its geological history records multiple cycles of rifting, passive margin sedimentation, island arc accretion, and continent-continent collision spanning over 2.5 billion years (A.M. Heron, 1953; A.B. Roy, 1988; G.B. Mahapatra, A Text-Book of Geology, Chapter 83, p. 312; P.K. Mukherjee, A Text Book of Geology, p. 200).

2. Stratigraphic Succession: Basement vs. Cover Relationships

In standard regional stratigraphy, the Precambrian rocks of the Aravalli Mountains are divided into the Archean Crystalline Basement and overlying Proterozoic supracrustal belts:

  1. 1. Archean Basement:
    • Banded Gneissic Complex (BGC) / Mewar Gneiss (Archean, >2.5 to 3.3 Ga): Composed of tonalite-trondhjemite-granodiorite (TTG) gneisses, amphibolites, pelitic schists, and intrusive multi-phase granitoids (e.g., Berach Granite ~2.5 Ga). This ancient gneissic floor forms the stable continental platform upon which the younger Proterozoic supracrustal basins opened.
  2. 2. Aravalli Supergroup (Paleoproterozoic, ~2.5 to 1.8 Ga): Separated from the underlying BGC by a profound angular unconformity marked by a basal conglomerate (the Debari / Delwara polymictic conglomerate). The supergroup represents an aborted continental rift evolving into a marine passive margin, classically subdivided from older to younger into three groups:
    • (a) Delwara Group (Basal): Represents the initial syn-rift volcanic-clastic stage, comprising amygdaloidal tholeiitic metabasalts (Delwara volcanics), quartzites, and basal polymictic conglomerates.
    • (b) Debari Group (Middle): Represents shelf and shallow-marine carbonate-arenite platform deposition. Composed of massive arkosic quartzites, conglomerates, carbonaceous phyllites, and dolomites. It is world-renowned for hosting the giant Jhamarkotra stromatolitic phosphorite deposit.
    • (c) Jharol Group (Upper): Represents deep-sea distal turbiditic sedimentation, characterized by a thick sequence of phyllites, mica schists, and thin-bedded quartzites with serpentinized ultramafic intrusions (ophiolitic fragments).
  3. 3. Delhi Supergroup (Mesoproterozoic, ~1.8 to 1.0 Ga): Rests with pronounced angular unconformity upon both the BGC and Aravalli rocks, divided into the Raialo, Alwar, and Ajabgarh groups.

Thus, the established temporal sequence from oldest to youngest is: Archean Basement [BGC] $\rightarrow$ Aravalli Supergroup [Delwara $\rightarrow$ Debari $\rightarrow$ Jharol], matching formula 1(d), 2(a, b, c).

Part II: Official Question & Critical Option Dissection

Q58. General geological formation of the Aravalli Mountains : [Group/Supergroup] Formation. Match the correct sequence from older to younger formations. The sequence is :
(A) 1(d), 2(a, b, c)
(B) 1(d), 2(c, b, a)
(C) 1(a), 2(b, c, d)
(D) None of these

Exam Question Analysis

Prompt: "General geological formation of the Aravalli Mountains : [Group/Supergroup] Formation. Match the correct sequence from older to younger formations. The sequence is : ________"

Option-by-Option Scientific Dissection

  • (A) 1(d), 2(a, b, c) (CORRECT): This represents the correct chronological and stratigraphic sequence from older to younger:
    • 1(d): The Archean Basement (1) is represented by the Banded Gneissic Complex (BGC) (d) (~3.3–2.5 Ga).
    • 2(a, b, c): The overlying Paleoproterozoic Aravalli Supergroup (2) develops in strict upward succession from basal syn-rift Delwara Group (a), to platform carbonate Debari Group (b), to deep-water turbiditic Jharol Group (c).
  • (B) 1(d), 2(c, b, a) (INCORRECT): This reverses the internal stratigraphy of the Aravalli Supergroup, erroneously placing the young, deep-marine Jharol Group at the base and the basal syn-rift Delwara volcanics at the top.
  • (C) 1(a), 2(b, c, d) (INCORRECT): Incorrectly assigns the Delwara Group to the Archean Basement and places the BGC into the cover sequence.
  • (D) None of these (INCORRECT): Option A is the rigorous, established sequence.
