Q24: Fold Geometry: The Hinge Line as the Line of Maximum Curvature

Exam: Geology Paper-II (2021)  |  Authoritative Reference: Haakon Fossen (Structural Geology, Ch. 10, pp. 214-218); Ramsay & Huber (Techniques of Modern Structural Geology, Vol. 2)

Part I: Theoretical Foundation & Concept Masterclass

24.1 Precise Terminology in Fold Geometry

In structural geology (Fleuty, 1964, The Description of Folds; Ramsay, 1967; Haakon Fossen, Structural Geology, Ch. 10, pp. 214–218), folded surfaces are rigorously characterized by specific mathematical lines and zones:

Fold Element Mathematical / Geometric Definition Orientation & Relationship to Topography
Hinge Line (Hinge) The locus of points of maximum curvature on a folded layer profile ($ rac{d^2y}{dx^2} = ext{max}$) Intrinsic structural property; completely independent of spatial or topographic orientation.
Fold Axis A geometric line which, when moved parallel to itself in space, generates the cylindrical fold form Direction vector of cylindrical symmetry; parallel to the hinge line in cylindrical folds.
Crest Line The line connecting the highest topographic / spatial points along a folded layer Depends on spatial orientation; coincides with the hinge line only in upright vertical folds.
Trough Line The line connecting the lowest topographic / spatial points along a folded layer Depends on spatial orientation; coincides with synclinal hinge only in upright folds.
Inflection Line The line separating opposite senses of curvature (zero curvature point, $\kappa = 0$) Separates the anticline from the syncline along the fold limb.

Part II: Official Question & Critical Option Dissection

Q24. The line of maximum curvature in a fold is known as —________.
(A) trough
(B) axis
(C) crest
(D) hinge
VERIFIED ANSWER: OPTION (D)

Correct Answer: (D) hinge — The line of maximum curvature on a folded rock surface is mathematically and structurally designated as the hinge (or hinge line).

Option-by-Option Critical Dissection

  • (A) trough — INCORRECT: The trough is the line of minimum structural elevation, which diverges from the hinge in inclined/plunging folds.
  • (B) axis — INCORRECT: The fold axis is an abstract directional vector generating a cylindrical fold by parallel translation, not an actual line of maximum curvature on the layer.
  • (C) crest — INCORRECT: The crest is the line connecting the highest points on the fold, which shifts away from the hinge line in asymmetrical and inclined folds.
  • (D) hinge — CORRECT: The canonical, universal structural definition established by M.J. Fleuty (1964) and J.G. Ramsay (1967).

Part III: Synthesis Reference Tables & High-Yield Practice

Fold Morphometrics Practice Problem

Practice Problem 24: Crest vs. Hinge in an Overturned Anticline

In an inclined or overturned anticline, do the crest line and the hinge line coincide? Explain why or why not.

Answer:

No, they do not coincide. In an inclined or overturned fold, the highest topographic point (the crest) shifts onto the upper limb, while the line of maximum mathematical curvature (the hinge) remains fixed at the structural nose of the fold. The crest and hinge only coincide in strictly upright folds with a vertical axial plane.
