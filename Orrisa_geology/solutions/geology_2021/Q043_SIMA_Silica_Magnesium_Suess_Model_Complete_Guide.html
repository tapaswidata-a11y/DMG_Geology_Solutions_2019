Q43: Earth's Geochemical Layering: Suess SIAL, SIMA & NIFE Model

Exam: Geology Paper-II (2021)  |  Authoritative Reference: Eduard Suess (Die Entstehung der Alpen, 1875); Arthur Holmes (Physical Geology); Bangar (Ch. 2)

Part I: Theoretical Foundation & Concept Masterclass

43.1 Eduard Suess (1875) Classical Compositional Model of Earth

In physical geology and historical geochemistry (Eduard Suess, 1875, Die Entstehung der Alpen; Holmes, Principles of Physical Geology; Bangar, Ch. 2), the Earth was first classified chemically into three concentric shells based on characteristic elemental abbreviations:

Historical Shell Name Chemical Element Roots Dominant Minerals & Rocks Mean Density ($\rho$) Modern Structural Equivalent
SIAL SIlica ($ ext{Si}$) + ALuminium ($ ext{Al}$) Quartz, K-feldspar, Na-plagioclase, Granite, Granodiorite $2.7 - 2.8\text{ g/cm}^3$ Upper Continental Crust (light, buoyant, forms granitic continents).
SIMA SIlica ($ ext{Si}$) + MAgnesium ($ ext{Mg}$) Pyroxene, Olivine, Ca-plagioclase, Basalt, Gabbro, Peridotite $2.9 - 3.4\text{ g/cm}^3$ Oceanic Crust, Lower Crust & Upper Mantle (denser, mafic substrate).
NIFE NIckel ($ ext{Ni}$) + FErrum / Iron ($ ext{Fe}$) Iron-Nickel metallic alloy ($80-85\%\text{ Fe}, 5-10\%\text{ Ni}$) $10.0 - 13.0\text{ g/cm}^3$ Earth's Core (Outer liquid core + Inner solid core).

43.2 Evolution to Modern Plate Tectonics

In Alfred Wegener's (1912) Continental Drift theory, the lighter granitic continents (SIAL) were envisioned as floating and plowing through the denser, plastic basaltic substrate of the oceanic floor (SIMA). Modern plate tectonics replaced this concept with rigid lithospheric plates (incorporating both crust and uppermost mantle) translating over the ductile asthenosphere.

Part II: Official Question & Critical Option Dissection

Q43. SIMA stands for:
(A) Silicon and Manganese
(B) Strontium and Magnesium
(C) Sulphur and Manganese
(D) Silicon and Magnesium
VERIFIED ANSWER: OPTION (D)

Correct Answer: (D) Silicon and Magnesium — In Eduard Suess's classical compositional nomenclature, SIMA stands for Silicon (Silica) and Magnesium.

Option-by-Option Critical Dissection

  • (A) Silicon and Manganese — INCORRECT: Manganese is a minor trace transition metal ($ ext{Mn}$), not a major rock-forming macro-element of the oceanic crust.
  • (B) Strontium and Magnesium — INCORRECT: Strontium ($ ext{Sr}$) is an incompatible trace element, not a major structural component of the silicate Earth.
  • (C) Sulphur and Manganese — INCORRECT: Completely misses silica, which forms the $45-52\%$ foundational backbone of mafic rocks.
  • (D) Silicon and Magnesium — CORRECT: The universal, canonical definition: SIlica ($ ext{Si}$) + MAgnesium ($ ext{Mg}$).
