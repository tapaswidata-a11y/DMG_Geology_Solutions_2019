Q22: Structural Geology: Attitude of Linear Features & Plunge Mechanics

Exam: Geology Paper-II (2021)  |  Authoritative Reference: Haakon Fossen (Structural Geology, Ch. 2, pp. 24-28); Davis & Reynolds (Structural Geology of Rocks & Regions, Ch. 1)

Part I: Theoretical Foundation & Concept Masterclass

22.1 Geometric Description of Linear vs. Planar Geological Features

In structural geology (Haakon Fossen, Structural Geology, Ch. 2, pp. 24–28; Davis & Reynolds, Structural Geology of Rocks and Regions, Ch. 1), the spatial orientation (attitude) of linear and planar elements is strictly distinguished:

Attitude Parameter Geometric Definition Applies To
Plunge The vertical angle between the horizontal plane and a line (or line of maximum elongation), measured downward in a vertical plane Linear features (fold hinge lines, slickenside lineations, mineral stretching lineations)
Trend The compass direction / azimuth of the vertical plane containing a plunging line Linear features
Dip Angle The acute vertical angle between the horizontal plane and an inclined planar surface Planar features (bedding, joints, faults, foliation)
Strike The compass bearing of a horizontal line contained within an inclined geological plane Planar features
Rake (Pitch) The angle between a line and the strike of the plane containing it, measured within the plane Linear features lying on a plane

Part II: Official Question & Critical Option Dissection

Q22. The vertical angle between the horizontal plane and the axis or line of maximum elongation of a feature is called ____.
(A) dip angle
(B) strike
(C) right-lateral strike slip
(D) plunge
VERIFIED ANSWER: OPTION (D)

Correct Answer: (D) plunge — The vertical angle between the horizontal plane and a linear feature or axis of maximum elongation is formally defined as the plunge.

Option-by-Option Critical Dissection

  • (A) dip angle — INCORRECT: Dip is the vertical angle of inclination of a planar surface (bedding or fault plane), not a linear feature.
  • (B) strike — INCORRECT: Strike is a horizontal compass direction on an inclined plane.
  • (C) right-lateral strike slip — INCORRECT: A kinematic fault displacement sense (dextral strike-slip), not an orientation angle.
  • (D) plunge — CORRECT: The precise structural definition for the vertical inclination of any linear geological element.

Structural Geometry Notation Practice

Practice Problem 22: Plunge / Trend Notation

A fold hinge line is recorded as $35^\circ \to 140^\circ$. Decode this structural measurement into its constituent plunge and trend components.

Answer:

  • Plunge: $35^\circ$ (inclined $35^\circ$ downwards from the horizontal plane).
  • Trend: $140^\circ$ (pointing towards South-East, azimuth $140^\circ$).
