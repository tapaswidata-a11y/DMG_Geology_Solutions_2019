Part I: Theoretical Foundation & Concept Masterclass

19.1 Payloads and Sensors of the IRS-1C / IRS-1D Satellite

In Indian space program history and sensor technology (ISRO NRSC Handbook; George Joseph, Fundamentals of Remote Sensing; Lillesand et al., Ch. 6), the IRS-1D (launched 29 September 1997 into an 821 km polar sun-synchronous orbit) carried three state-of-the-art optical payloads:

Sensor Name Sensor Type & Spectral Bands Spatial Resolution Swath Width & Revisit
PAN (Panchromatic Camera) Single visible band ($0.50 - 0.75\ \mu\text{m}$) $5.8\text{ meters}$ (steerable off-nadir up to $\pm 26^\circ$) $70\text{ km}$ ($24\text{ days}$)
LISS-III (Linear Imaging Self-Scanning Sensor III) Multispectral: 4 bands
• B2 (Green: $0.52 - 0.59\ \mu\text{m}$)
• B3 (Red: $0.62 - 0.68\ \mu\text{m}$)
• B4 (NIR: $0.77 - 0.86\ \mu\text{m}$)
• B5 (SWIR: $1.55 - 1.70\ \mu\text{m}$)		 $23.5\text{ meters}$ (VNIR Bands 2, 3, 4)
($70.5\text{ meters}$ for SWIR Band 5)		 $141\text{ km}$ ($24\text{ days}$)
WiFS (Wide Field Sensor) 2 bands (Red: $0.62-0.68\ \mu\text{m}$, NIR: $0.77-0.86\ \mu\text{m}$) $188\text{ meters}$ $810\text{ km}$ ($5\text{ days}$)

Part II: Official Question & Critical Option Dissection

Q19. In IRS 1D satellite, the spatial resolution of MSS sensor is :
(A) 1m.
(B) 30m
(C) 79m
(D) 23.5m
VERIFIED ANSWER: OPTION (D)

Correct Answer: (D) 23.5m — The primary multispectral scanner (LISS-III) onboard the IRS-1D satellite has a spatial resolution of 23.5 meters across its VNIR bands.

Option-by-Option Critical Dissection

  • (A) 1m — INCORRECT: 1-meter resolution belongs to modern high-resolution commercial satellites like IKONOS / Cartosat-2.
  • (B) 30m — INCORRECT: 30 meters is the canonical spatial resolution of the NASA Landsat Thematic Mapper (TM/ETM+/OLI).
  • (C) 79m — INCORRECT: 79 meters (nominal 80m) is the spatial resolution of the legacy Landsat Multispectral Scanner (MSS).
  • (D) 23.5m — CORRECT: The hallmark spatial resolution of ISRO's IRS-1C and IRS-1D LISS-III multispectral sensor.

Practice Problem 19: Sensor Comparison Matrix

Match the satellite sensor to its exact spatial resolution: (1) Landsat-8 OLI Multispectral, (2) Sentinel-2 MSI VNIR, (3) IRS-1D LISS-III VNIR, (4) Cartosat-1 PAN.

Answer:

  • (1) Landsat-8 OLI Multispectral = $30\text{ meters}$
  • (2) Sentinel-2 MSI VNIR = $10\text{ meters}$
  • (3) IRS-1D LISS-III VNIR = $23.5\text{ meters}$
  • (4) Cartosat-1 PAN = $2.5\text{ meters}$
