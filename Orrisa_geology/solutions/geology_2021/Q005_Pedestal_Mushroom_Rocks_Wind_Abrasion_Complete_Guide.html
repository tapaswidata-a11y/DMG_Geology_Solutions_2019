Q5: Pedestal Rocks & Mushroom Rocks (Differential Wind Abrasion)

Exam: Geology Paper-II (2021)  |  Authoritative Reference: Arthur Holmes (Principles of Physical Geology); K.M. Bangar (Engineering Geology, Ch. 5, pp. 78-82); G.B. Mahapatra

Part I: Theoretical Foundation & Concept Masterclass

5.1 Genesis of Pedestal & Mushroom Rocks

In arid geomorphology (Arthur Holmes, Principles of Physical Geology; K.M. Bangar, Principles of Engineering Geology, Ch. 5), isolated rock outcrops exhibiting a slender basal stem supporting an expanded, mushroom-like cap are termed pedestal rocks or mushroom rocks (German: Pilzfelsen).

Morphology Slender neck/column supporting an overarching wider cap Rounded corestone or boulder poised delicately on a bedrock pedestal Alien boulder resting abruptly on bedrock or high ridge

5.2 Physics of Saltation and Basal Undercutting

Wind-driven sand transport occurs overwhelmingly via saltation. Heavy quartz sand grains rarely bounce higher than $1\text{ to }1.5\text{ meters}$ above the desert floor. Consequently, wind abrasion (sandblasting) reaches maximum kinetic intensity near ground level, actively undercutting rock bases into slender necks while higher portions remain uncarved.

Part II: Official Question & Critical Option Dissection

Q5. An isolated erosional rock mass comprising a slender stem, neck or column supporting a wider cap is known as: (i) Pedestal rocks, (ii) Mushroom rocks, (iii) Balanced rocks, (iv) Perched blocks. Choose the correct set of answer from the following options :
(A) (i)
(B) (i) and (ii)
(C) (i), (ii) and (iii)
(D) (i), (ii), (iii) and (iv)
VERIFIED ANSWER: OPTION (B)

Correct Answer: (B) (i) and (ii) — An isolated rock mass comprising a slender stem supporting a wider cap is synonymously designated as Pedestal rocks and Mushroom rocks.

Option-by-Option Critical Dissection

  • (i) Pedestal rocks — TRUE: Standard geomorphic term for mushroom-shaped undercut residual rock towers.
  • (ii) Mushroom rocks — TRUE: Universal colloquial and academic synonym (Pilzfelsen).
  • (iii) Balanced rocks — FALSE: Balanced rocks lack a slender carved erosional stem; they are tor blocks or corestones perched on tiny contact points.
  • (iv) Perched blocks — FALSE: Perched blocks are glacial erratics stranded atop foreign bedrock during ice retreat.

Concept Check: Ventifacts vs. Yardangs vs. Zeugen

Practice Problem 5: Comparative Aeolian Erosional Landforms

Distinguish between a Zeugen and a Yardang in terms of bedrock structure and wind alignment.

Answer:

  • Zeugen: Formed on horizontally bedded strata where a hard, resistant rock layer caps an underlying soft rock layer. Wind opens joints into ridge-and-furrow table forms.
  • Yardang: Formed on vertically or steeply tilted alternating hard and soft strata that run parallel to the prevailing wind direction, carved into streamlined, keel-like ridges.
