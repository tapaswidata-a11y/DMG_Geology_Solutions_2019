Part I: Theoretical Foundation & Concept Masterclass

37.1 Global Tectonic Framework of Ocean Basins

In marine geology and global plate tectonics (C.M.R. Fowler, The Solid Earth: An Introduction to Global Geophysics, 2nd Ed., Ch. 2; Kearey, Klepeis & Vine, Global Tectonics, 3rd Ed., Ch. 4), the ocean floor is subdivided into divergent mid-ocean ridge systems, convergent subduction trenches, and transform fracture zones:

Feature Name Geotectonic Nature Actual Geographic & Ocean Basin Location Associated Tectonic Plates
Reykjanes Ridge Divergent Mid-Ocean Spreading Ridge (Segment of Mid-Atlantic Ridge) North Atlantic Ocean (southwest of Iceland, between $55^\circ\text{N}$ and $63^\circ\text{N}$). North American Plate & Eurasian Plate
Carlsberg Ridge Slow-spreading Divergent Mid-Ocean Ridge Northwestern Indian Ocean (trending SE from Gulf of Aden / Socotra towards Chagos). African Plate (Somali) & Indo-Australian Plate
Kermadec Trench Deep-sea Convergent Subduction Trench ($10,047\text{ m}$ depth) Southwestern Pacific Ocean (north of New Zealand, continuous with Tonga Trench). Pacific Plate subducting beneath Indo-Australian Plate
Phoenix Ridge (Antarctic-Phoenix) Extinct Spreading Ridge Segment Southern Pacific / Antarctic Ocean (Drake Passage / Antarctic Peninsula, NOT the Arctic Ocean!). Former Phoenix Plate & Antarctic Plate
Gakkel Ridge Ultra-slow Divergent Mid-Ocean Ridge True Arctic Ocean Spreading Ridge (between Greenland and Siberia). North American Plate & Eurasian Plate

37.2 The Arctic Ocean Spreading Center: Gakkel Ridge

The sole active spreading mid-ocean ridge in the Arctic Ocean is the Gakkel Ridge (formerly Nansen Cordillera), the slowest spreading center on Earth ($< 10\text{ mm/yr}$). The Phoenix Ridge is located at the antipodal southern extremity of the globe near the South Shetland Trench in Antarctica.

Part II: Official Question & Critical Option Dissection

Q37. Find the incorrect pair :
(A) Kermadec Trench — Pacific Ocean
(B) Reykjanes Ridge — Atlantic Ocean
(C) Phoenix Ridge — Arctic Ocean
(D) Carlsberg Ridge — Indian Ocean
VERIFIED ANSWER: OPTION (C)

Correct Answer: (C) Phoenix Ridge — Arctic Ocean — This pair is incorrect. The Phoenix Ridge is located in the Antarctic / Southern Pacific Ocean near the Antarctic Peninsula, whereas the mid-ocean ridge of the Arctic Ocean is the Gakkel Ridge.

Option-by-Option Critical Dissection

  • (A) Kermadec Trench — Pacific Ocean — CORRECT PAIR: The Kermadec Trench is one of the deepest subduction zones on Earth, located in the South Pacific Ocean.
  • (B) Reykjanes Ridge — Atlantic Ocean — CORRECT PAIR: The Reykjanes Ridge is the prominent northern arm of the Mid-Atlantic Ridge extending directly to Iceland.
  • (C) Phoenix Ridge — Arctic Ocean — INCORRECT PAIR (The Required Answer): The Phoenix Ridge is an extinct spreading center in the Pacific-Antarctic/Drake Passage region of Antarctica, not the Arctic Ocean.
  • (D) Carlsberg Ridge — Indian Ocean — CORRECT PAIR: The Carlsberg Ridge is the principal NW-SE spreading ridge bisecting the northern Indian Ocean.
