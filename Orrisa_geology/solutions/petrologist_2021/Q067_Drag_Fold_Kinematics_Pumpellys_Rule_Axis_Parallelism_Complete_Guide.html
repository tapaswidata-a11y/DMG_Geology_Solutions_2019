🔄 Drag Fold Kinematics & Pumpelly's Rule

Discipline: Structural Geology & Tectonics Paper Code: GPGM - 3/20 Source: OPSC Petrologist Examination
Official Examination Question
Which of the following is not true in case of drag fold ?
(A) The axes of the drag folds are parallel to those of major folds
(B) The drag folds plunge in the same manner as the major folds
(C) The layers on the upper side of the drag fold slides away from synclincal axis
(D) The axes of the drag folds are not parallel to those of the major folds '
Part I

Theoretical Principles & Core Geology Foundations

1. Fundamental Geological Principles & Core Concepts

This question examines Drag Fold Kinematics & Pumpelly's Rule in the discipline of Structural Geology & Tectonics.

In standard geological investigations, the established governing principles demonstrate that: The axes of the drag folds are not parallel to those of the major folds (Pumpelly's Rule dictates that minor drag fold axes ARE strictly parallel to major fold axes).

Standard Reference Axiom:

Haakon Fossen (Structural Geology, Ch. 11, pp. 240-245); Ramsay & Huber

Part II

Forensic Option Dissection & Error Analysis

Option (A): The axes of the drag folds are parallel to those of major folds — INCORRECT

Does not satisfy the primary diagnostic criteria for this geological phenomenon.

Option (B): The drag folds plunge in the same manner as the major folds — INCORRECT

Represents an alternative or contrasting geological classification.

Option (C): The layers on the upper side of the drag fold slides away from synclincal axis — INCORRECT

Not the appropriate classification under the established stratigraphic and petrological guidelines.

Option (D): The axes of the drag folds are not parallel to those of the major folds ' — CORRECT ANSWER

Accurately satisfies the foundational geological definition and authoritative exam criteria.
