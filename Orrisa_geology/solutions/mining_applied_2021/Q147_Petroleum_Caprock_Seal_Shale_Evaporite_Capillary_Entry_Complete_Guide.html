The layer containing petroleum oil and gas is :
(A) Above that of water

(D) Belowsand
Part I

Applied Geology & Mining Engineering Foundations

1. Applied Geology & Mining Engineering Foundations

This problem evaluates Caprock Sealing Integrity in Petroleum Geology within the framework of Economic Geology & Mineral Deposits.

In standard geological and mining operations, the definitive verified conclusion establishes that: Ductile, thick, impermeable shale or bedded halite with high capillary entry pressure preventing buoyant hydrocarbon leakage.
