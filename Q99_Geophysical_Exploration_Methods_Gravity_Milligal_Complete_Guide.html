Question 99: Geophysical Methods: Units, Density Contrasts & Magnetics

Systematic Analysis of Geophysical Properties, Unit Definitions, and Identifying the Incorrect Statement

Q.99: Which one of the following statements regarding geophysical exploration methods is NOT correct?

✓ (a) Milligal is a unit of magnetic field.
(b) Magnetic anomaly can be caused by pyrrhotite and magnetite.
(c) Gravity method of mineral exploration is based on density contrasts.
(d) Scintillometers can detect radioactivity in rocks.
Correct Option: (a) Milligal is a unit of magnetic field. (Statement is NOT correct)

1. Analysis of Statement (a): Units of Gravity vs. Magnetism

The statement that "Milligal is a unit of magnetic field" is factually incorrect:

  • The Gal & Milligal: The gal (symbol Gal, named in honor of Galileo Galilei) is the CGS unit of gravitational acceleration:
    1 Gal = 1 cm/s2 = 10−2 m/s2
    1 milligal (mGal) = 10−3 Gal = 10−5 m/s2 ≈ 10−6 g
    Gravitational anomalies caused by mineral deposits, salt domes, and faults are typically measured in tenths to tens of milligals (mGal) or gravity units (1 g.u. = 0.1 mGal = 1 μm/s2).
  • Magnetic Units: The magnetic field in geophysics (magnetic flux density B) is measured in Tesla (T) or nanoTesla (nT) in SI units, or traditionally in gamma (γ) and Gauss (G):
    1 nT = 1 γ = 10−9 T = 10−5 Gauss
    Earth's total geomagnetic field ranges from ~25,000 nT near the magnetic equator to ~65,000 nT near the magnetic poles.

2. Verification of Correct Statements (b, c, d)

  • Statement (b) is CORRECT: Both magnetite (Fe3O4) and monoclinic pyrrhotite (Fe7S8) are ferrimagnetic minerals possessing very high magnetic susceptibility and remanent magnetization. Pyrrhotite is a common constituent of nickel-copper sulfide deposits (e.g., Sudbury) and produces massive magnetic anomalies second only to magnetite.
  • Statement (c) is CORRECT: The gravity method detects subsurface anomalies by measuring spatial variations in the gravitational field caused by lateral density contrasts (Δρ = ρtarget − ρhost). High-density bodies like chromite, barite, and massive sulfides produce positive Bouguer gravity anomalies, while low-density salt domes produce negative anomalies.
  • Statement (d) is CORRECT: A scintillometer is a radiometric instrument containing a thallium-activated sodium iodide crystal [NaI(Tl)] that emits light flashes (scintillations) when struck by gamma-ray photons emitted during the decay of radioactive isotopes (238U, 232Th, 40K).

3. Systematic Option Evaluation

Option Statement Evaluated Factual Correctness Status as Answer to Question
(a) Milligal is a unit of magnetic field. INCORRECT. Milligal (10−5 m/s2) is the standard unit of gravitational acceleration, whereas magnetic field is measured in nanoTesla (nT) or gamma. Correct Choice (Identifies the incorrect statement)
(b) Magnetic anomaly can be caused by pyrrhotite and magnetite. CORRECT. Magnetite and monoclinic pyrrhotite are strongly ferrimagnetic minerals that generate strong regional and local magnetic anomalies. Incorrect Choice
(c) Gravity method of mineral exploration is based on density contrasts. CORRECT. The fundamental operating physical property of gravity prospecting is the lateral density contrast (Δρ) between the orebody and country rock. Incorrect Choice
(d) Scintillometers can detect radioactivity in rocks. CORRECT. Scintillometers detect gamma-ray radiation from uranium, thorium, and potassium in radioactive prospecting. Incorrect Choice

Key Geophysical Takeaway

In applied geophysics:
• Gravity: Unit = Milligal (mGal) or μm/s2 • Property = Density (ρ)
• Magnetics: Unit = nanoTesla (nT) or gamma (γ) • Property = Magnetic Susceptibility (κ)
